# Wanderer Chat 1.3.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (1.4 or later). The chat moves here from Wanderer, with your settings and conversations.

### New
- The glass look: no frame at rest, each line on a light veil that fades (after 20 seconds, 1 or 2 minutes, or never), everything back under the mouse or while you type. Each line reads at a glance: the time, one letter for the chat, the name in its color. Scrolled up, one click brings you back to the latest.
- Your name in gold, and your other names too (a nickname, your main's); the tab where you are named gets a gold dot. In the General tab, your guild and your group stand out a little. A quiet sound for your name if you wish, once a minute at most.
- The alerts beside the chat, moved with Wanderer's placing mode: a card when you are named where you could not read it (another tab in front, the chat faded, you busy), with a button to answer.
- Cards for a greeting or a goodbye to the guild, answered by name as the hour wants, sent only by your Enter. A whisper not answered yet waits in a small line above the chat.
- Quieter public channels: the same words again within a minute and obvious gold selling kept out, never your lines, friends, guild, group or whispers. Words of your own can keep lines out too.
- Gold selling whispered by a stranger: no sound, no window. Everything kept out waits in one list, shown in one click and ready to copy.
- In the public channels, when you ask: a card for a group sought for a dungeon of your level, or for a word you watch.
- Tabs in one click: Group, Guild, Whispers and Channels beside General, each counting what came while it was hidden.
- A search of the tab: the words found in gold, Enter for the older one, Shift+Enter for the newer.
- A card on player names: level, class, zone, guild and rank, a friend or one of your characters, your private note. Their menu gains Greet, Reply, Quote and Copy the line.
- The comforts: sent messages with the arrow keys (kept between sessions), a copy button, each tab's last lines kept after a reload, web addresses as links, one text size for every window, shorter channel names.
- Private messages in their own window (an option, offered once): one conversation per person in bubbles, pinned or searched, your unsent words kept in each conversation, your colors for each side.
- A key answers the last one who spoke to you.
- Steps aside for ElvUI, Prat, Chattynator, Glass and WIM where they do the same. Everything kept per character. In ten languages.
