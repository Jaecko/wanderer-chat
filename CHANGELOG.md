# Wanderer Chat 1.3.28

### New
- Private messages: when someone is away, busy or not online, the game's answer shows in their conversation instead of the chat; a message that never left stays faded.
- Private messages: a conversation opens with a "New" line before what you had not read yet, and the window opens on the newest conversation waiting for you.
- Private messages: a search opens the conversation on the message found, its edge in gold.
- Private messages: a command typed in the answer line (/dance, /g hello) goes to the chat line, never whispered; a click beside the messages puts you in the answer line; web addresses in a message become links to copy; a veiled message comes with its words when the chat unveils lines.

### Fixed
- Hidden lines window: a right click on a name opens the game's player menu (report included), a click whispers.
- Whispers kept out of the chat flash the game's icon in the taskbar again.
- Glass look: during a fight the tabs come back under the mouse, so you can switch chat windows.
- Glass reads every line again when the game shows the time: guild, party and say lines were left as the game made them, with their [Guild] and brackets.
- The guild message of the day already seen really leaves the chat at login.
- A link clicked in the messages window or in the hidden lines no longer gets Wanderer Chat blocked by the game: an item, a spell or a quest opens its tooltip (a second click closes it), Shift puts it in the chat, Ctrl tries an item on; an invitation to a community or a guild, which only the game opens, is given to your chat window, where a click opens it.
- R and /r reply again to the last whisper while the messages window is on; Shift and a click put a link in its reply line.
- The gold sellers' filter no longer hides ordinary lines like '20 gold. Come to IF'.
- The channel letter in the glass look is a link again: a click to write in that channel, a right click for its menu.
- The key list shows Private messages once; a key set on the old entry is moved over.
- A rare error could leave new chat lines dimmed and not kept until the next reload.

### Changed
- The dot saying you were named on a chat tab is larger and stays visible at rest; Stop ignoring shows an undo sign.
- Glass keeps the raid and party leader and raid warning marks, and the color of yells and raid warnings.
- The conversations list scrolls with the wheel; old ones forgotten are said; removing a friend asks first.
- Escape closes the Messages window like every other window; while you write, it first leaves the line.
- The answer line brings back what you sent with Up and Down, and Tab moves to the next person you talked with.
- The search and hidden lines windows play their sounds; "Nothing found." as everywhere.
- /wchat dump also follows your guild lines, to find a missing one.
- The tabs no longer show a count of the lines that came while hidden: it floated over the game when the chat faded, for little use. The gold dot when a line names you stays.

### Removed
- The card that appeared when someone greeted the guild or said goodbye, more bothersome than useful.
