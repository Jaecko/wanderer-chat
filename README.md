# Wanderer Chat

Presence without taking room: the chat stays the one place to read, and only what is for you comes up beside it. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- The alerts, beside the chat (moved with Wanderer's placing mode): your name said where you could not read it (another tab in front, the chat faded, you busy), with a button to answer.
- In the chat: a line that names you (or one of the other names you gave: a nickname, your main) is in gold, and its tab gets a small gold dot; in the General tab, your guild and your group stand out a little. A sound for your name, when asked for.
- The cards, only to act: someone greets the guild or says goodbye (one button writes an answer by name, as the hour wants; your Enter sends it). Nothing is ever sent without your own Enter.
- The public channels, when asked for: a group sought for a dungeon of your level, or a word you chose.
- Kept out, never lost: repeats and gold selling in the public channels, the lines holding a word you chose to hide, gold selling whispered by a stranger (no sound, no window); all in one list to show them.
- The tabs: one click makes Group, Guild, Whispers and Channels beside General; each tab counts what your guild, your group and your whispers said while it was not shown.
- The private messages in their own window, the conversation as a thread of bubbles, your unsent words kept in each conversation.
- The chat's comforts: your sent messages with the arrow keys, a copy button and a search of the tab (the words found in gold), a line copied from the menu of a name, each tab's lines kept between sessions, web addresses as links, one text size for every window.
- A key answers the last one who spoke to you.

Settings in Wanderer's options; /wchat opens the messages.

More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
